Our assessment · what's already exceptional 02

Four things here are rare. We build around them.

01 · The evidence architecture

Modelled. Targeted. Measured.

Performance claims published with a grammar of honesty, and as-built results promised once testing completes. No competitor practice does this. It is the digital expression of "a beautiful house can still perform poorly as a home."

"Your first conversation is with Roger."

Roger opens every engagement and sets the design direction. 03 · The lived-experience voice

The client on morning light

"We understood the drawings, but we didn't fully understand what those decisions would mean until we lived here." The proof layer, working. Captured systematically, this outweighs any interaction novelty.

04 · Care beyond completion

Systems, processes and lifestyle measurement

The processes that keep measuring how the home performs and how life in it feels after move-in. They protect livability for decades, and they mean clients experience BA as invested in the home well past handover. This is the foundation the living records stand on, and nobody else in the market holds it.

Our assessment · where we'd push 03

The differentiator is buried.

The evidence architecture, the site's most defensible material, currently sits six sections deep, below content any competitor could have written. The hierarchy should be inverted: "this practice measures what others only claim" legible within two scrolls.

A

The Homes gallery is the most generic element

A uniform grid of named houses is the default of the entire category. We'd build hierarchy into it, and whisper one measured outcome per card, so even the gallery shows the homes are proven.

B

The homepage carries the whole argument

Well-ordered but long, with sections competing at similar weight. Progressive disclosure, your own principle, argues for a shorter page that opens doors and lets the visitor choose their depth.

C

Typographic risk at small sizes

Cormorant is right for the register and fragile below display sizes. We'd confine it strictly to display use, with a tested scale and loading behaviour so the serif never renders broken.

The signature move 04

Make evidence the device the whole site speaks in.

One consistent visual grammar for modelled, targeted and measured, present from the gallery card to the project page to the Method page. Every practice in this market has photography. Only BA has proof with a publishing standard. The site should make that impossible to miss and effortless to understand.

In the gallery

One measured outcome whispered per card, where it exists. The browsing layer starts the argument.

On the project page

Outcome → decision → consequence, with Inside the Work as its own visual register for drawings, thinking and evidence.

Over time

Evidence stored as structured data, so completed homes become living records: measured energy, comfort, ageing, client observation. The strongest authority play available to BA.

The research behind the recommendations 05

Forty practices. Winners and strugglers. A falsifiable matrix.

We are running a structured study of $5m-tier residential practice websites, Australian and global: ten winning practices and ten stalled ones in each market, selected on business signals before their websites are examined, then torn down against a weighted ten-criterion scoring matrix.

The matrix and reference set follow. The full forty-practice teardown, screenshots and scores included, is argued at the Design Development Review inside month one.

01

The category converges hard

Uniform project grids, "Contact" as the only ask, credentials pages nobody reads. Convergence is the opportunity: the evidence device has no precedent in the cohort.

02

Winners sell the principal's thinking

The practices commanding the top tier make the principal's convictions unmistakable. Confidence reads before credentials do.

03

Strugglers decay visibly

Stale work, dead journals, generic positioning. A $5m prospect reads site neglect as practice neglect within seconds.

The experience and the platform 09

Effortless in front. Disciplined underneath.

01

Homes interaction

Gallery → project → Inside the Work, with state preserved: return lands at your gallery position, previous and next move between projects. Every layer skippable. Each project one substantive, crawlable, AI-legible document.

02

Mobile carries the first visit

Most first visits arrive from a referral on a phone. Photography full-bleed and fast, evidence as clean expandable units, Core Web Vitals green on mid-range hardware, verified on real devices before launch.

03

Capture built in, execution with your team

Enquiry capture, segmentation and source tracking are built into the site and wired to analytics. Antimony plans and reviews the email journeys that run off it. The BA team sends them.

04

Migration as protection

Full URL audit, one-to-one redirect mapping, staged launch, post-launch crawls and a 72-hour redirect watch. Existing search authority is an asset, and the migration protocol carries every ranking page across.

Platform selection

Due diligence before commitment

We will not name the platform before discovery. Month one includes a structured platform and CMS assessment against your brief's own criteria: design fidelity, day-to-day usability for the BA team, semantic and structured-data control, performance ceiling, portability and total cost. You get the shortlist, the scoring and a recommendation with its trade-offs stated. Selection is a Gate 1 decision, made together.

CMS as foundation

Evidence stored as structured data

Whichever platform is selected, evidence is stored as structured data: metric, modelled, targeted, measured, test date, status. This is what makes living records, semantic search and any future conversational layer possible without a rebuild.

Future-facing, judged by your own threshold 10

AI works behind the site. We co-sign your list.

Build now

Invisible AI in the workflow: assisted alt text, metadata and schema, human-approved always. Related-content suggestion. Content QA against staleness. Search Console interpretation feeding quarterly decisions.

Architect for later

The structured content model, entity-clear markup and clean API surface that let living project records, semantic search and conversational access attach later without rebuilding. The 100 Year Home prototyped as the simplest form that genuinely explains time, and shipped only if it beats the essay.

Not building

Chatbots at launch, configurators, gamification, visible AI, intrusive personalisation, scroll-hijacking, AI-generated imagery near real projects, and any enquiry mechanism that makes a $5m prospect feel processed.

The threshold stays yours: every interaction earns its place by reducing effort, improving understanding or increasing confidence, or it ships as nothing.
